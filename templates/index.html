📖 How to Use Audio Analysis

Analyze Recording/Audio: Extracts musical features (tempo, energy, key, etc.) from your audio to find similar songs based on sound characteristics.

Identify Song: Recognizes what song is playing by matching it against music databases. Use this to discover the name and artist of unknown tracks.

🎯 Best Practices for Song Identification:

  • Use 15-30 seconds of clear audio from the song
  • Choose a distinctive part (chorus works best)
  • Minimize background noise and talking
  • Ensure good audio quality (avoid distortion)
  • For recordings, hold device close to the audio source
