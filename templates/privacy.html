Privacy Policy for MelodySearch

Last Updated: December 21, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to MelodySearch. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our music discovery and mood board application ("MelodySearch" or "the Service").

By using MelodySearch, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Spotify Data

When you connect your Spotify account to MelodySearch, we access the following information through the Spotify API:

  • Your Spotify profile information (username, email)
  • Your currently playing track
  • Your recently played tracks
  • Your saved library (songs, albums, artists)
  • Playback state (what you're listening to, device information)

2.2 Pinterest Data

When you use our mood board feature with Pinterest integration, we may access:

  • Public Pinterest images related to your music interests
  • Artist and album-related visual content

2.3 Unsplash Data

We use the Unsplash API to fetch aesthetic imagery for mood boards. No personal data is collected through Unsplash integration.

2.4 Local Storage

We store the following data locally in your browser:

  • Saved mood boards
  • User preferences and settings
  • Authentication tokens (for Spotify session management)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Display your currently playing music
  • Show your recently played tracks
  • Generate personalized mood boards based on your listening history
  • Create visual collections related to your music taste
  • Save your library and collections for easy access
  • Improve and personalize your experience with MelodySearch

4. Data Storage and Security

We do NOT store your data on our servers. All data is either:

  • Temporarily accessed from Spotify, Pinterest, or Unsplash APIs during your session
  • Stored locally in your browser's local storage
  • Cleared when you log out or clear your browser data

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including secure HTTPS connections and token-based authentication.

5. Third-Party Services

MelodySearch integrates with the following third-party services:

5.1 Spotify

We use the Spotify Web API to access your music data. Spotify's privacy policy applies: https://www.spotify.com/privacy

5.2 Pinterest

We use the Pinterest API to fetch visual content for mood boards. Pinterest's privacy policy applies: https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy

5.3 Unsplash

We use the Unsplash API for high-quality aesthetic imagery. Unsplash's privacy policy applies: https://unsplash.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We only share data with:

  • Spotify, Pinterest, and Unsplash APIs as necessary to provide the Service
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities if required by law

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View what data we access from your Spotify account
  • Disconnect: Revoke MelodySearch's access to your Spotify account at any time through your Spotify account settings
  • Delete: Clear all locally stored data by clearing your browser's local storage or logging out
  • Opt-out: Stop using the Service at any time

8. Children's Privacy

MelodySearch is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. International Users

MelodySearch is hosted and operated from various locations. If you are accessing the Service from outside these locations, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in locations where our servers are located.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

12. Data Retention

Since we do not store your data on our servers, data retention is managed by:

  • Your browser: Local storage data persists until you clear it or log out
  • Third-party APIs: Spotify, Pinterest, and Unsplash retain data according to their own policies

13. Cookies and Tracking

MelodySearch uses minimal cookies and local storage to:

  • Maintain your login session
  • Remember your preferences
  • Store authentication tokens

We do NOT use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

14. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using your information includes:

  • Consent: You consent to our use of Spotify, Pinterest, and Unsplash APIs when you connect your accounts
  • Legitimate Interest: To provide and improve the Service
